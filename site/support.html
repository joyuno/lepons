FAQ

My phone can't reach my PC.
Make sure the PC is on, the Lepons PC app is running, and phone access is turned on in the PC app settings.

How do I pair again?
Unpair in the phone app, then scan the new QR code shown in the PC app.

Where do I get the PC app?
From GitHub Releases.

Is the source code available?
Yes. Lepons is open source under the MIT License. Read it, change it and reuse it on GitHub.
