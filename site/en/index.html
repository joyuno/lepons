Share a codebase

The AI on another computer reads your code without you copying it over.

Drag a folder onto another computer in the sharing map. That's it. The AI there finds and reads the files it needs, so you spend less time pasting code and explaining it.

The receiving AI only reads. Changes are handed to the AI on the computer that has the folder, and that handed-off work runs without permission prompts, so share only between computers you trust. When it reads your code, values that look like passwords or keys are hidden.

Carry on from your phone

Leave your desk without leaving the AI stuck.

You get a notification when an AI puts up a choice for you. Read the conversation on your phone and answer, and the PC carries on. Data is encrypted before it goes through the relay server, and the relay server neither reads nor stores it.

The PC has to be on with the PC app running. One PC connects to one phone, and the phone cannot quit an AI.

Manage servers and PCs

Open a server the way you open a folder on your PC.

Enter its address and Lepons connects over SSH. Nothing gets installed on the remote machine. If the small program that keeps the AI going when the connection drops (tmux) is missing, one button installs it.
